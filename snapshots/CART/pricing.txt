Frequently asked questions
What is Instacart+?
You don’t need a membership to shop with Instacart. But if you want to save time and money, Instacart+ (formerly known as Instacart Express) can help. It’s a membership program that offers exclusive benefits ($0 delivery fee on grocery and retail orders of $10+, Costco orders of $35+, and eligible restaurant orders of $25+. Service fees apply)
How much does Instacart+ cost?
For a flat standard fee of $99/year or $9.99/month, members enjoy exclusive benefits ($0 delivery fee on grocery and retail orders of $10+, Costco orders of $35+, and eligible restaurant orders of $25+. Service fees apply)
How can Instacart+ save me money?
If you order just twice a month, the membership can pay for itself.
What’s the difference between Instacart Express and Instacart+?
Instacart Express is now Instacart+! It’s still the best way to get the most out of Instacart, but with a new name and look, and added benefits designed for you and your family.
Active subscribers are now Instacart+ members. Please note that you may still see references to Instacart Express on the Instacart Platform. This can happen if your app hasn't been updated, or if you visit a page that has not yet been updated.
