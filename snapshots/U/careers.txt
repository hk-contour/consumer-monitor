ALERT: Unity has received reports of scams where individuals purporting to be Unity HR representatives conduct bogus employment interviews via email or text, and then request payment as a condition for receiving an offer of employment. Please be aware that Unity does not conduct interviews by email or text, and will never request payment as a condition for applying for a position or receiving an offer of employment. These scam operators may also ask for your personal information (name, address, birthdate, social security number, etc.) which you should not provide to them. If you have been a target of such a scam, you should report it by contacting the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (see this FTC posting for further details), the office of your state Attorney General, or the government agency responsible for investigating matters such as this where you reside.

Frequently asked questions
Your questions answered!
What are Unity's Principles?
Unity’s Principles are the foundation of our ever-evolving culture. They guide how we navigate ambiguity, solve problems together, and stay grounded when things get tough. These Principles are designed to align us on how we work - how we show up for each other, make decisions, and shape Unity into a company we’re proud to be part of.
Our four principles are:
Lead with empathy and respect.
Communicate with candor.
Act with urgency.
Prioritize the greater good.
Read more about our Principles and how we live them
here
.
Do you have an employee referral program?
We absolutely do! We encourage you to reach out to any friends or former colleagues who work at Unity so that they can submit your information for the role that you are interested in.
I applied previously and wasn’t successful. Can I reapply?
You absolutely can. If you see another position that is a good match for your experience, skills, and interests, then we encourage you to apply again.
