A buyer clicks “Contact agent” or "Request a Tour"
Across Zillow, Trulia, StreetEasy, and Hotpads, the buyer fills out their contact info to start the process or requests a tour.
We verify they are looking to buy
Buyers who are connected live are vetted based on their timeline to buy.
We connect you to them
We transfer buyers who indicated they are ready to speak with you or schedule a tour. When available, we pass their tour request details directly for Real-Time Touring connections.
You are their “go-to” agent
After the call or confirmed tour, you get a My Agent relationship where we assign that buyer to you for all listings across Zillow and a snapshot of the buyer’s key details.
And get client insight tools
When you get a My Agent relationship with these buyers, you unlock actionable insights about their browsing activity on Zillow, empowering you to provide superior service.

Agents often ask:
What is Zillow Premier Agent?
Zillow Premier Agent connects real estate professionals with buyers and sellers. You’ll receive connections through live calls, emails, tours, and pre-approved buyers. For a comprehensive overview, visit our
Zillow Premier Agent 101 page
.
How much does Zillow advertising cost? / How much does Zillow Premier Agent cost?
The price for Zillow Premier Agent advertising varies by market, home values, expected connections, and more. The average cost per connection in major metro areas is $223 and $139 in non-major metros. For a personalized cost estimate connect with a Zillow business advisor to create a tailored Premier Agent package.
Is being a Premier Agent worth it?
Yes, for many realtors, Zillow Premier Agent is worth it. Zillow connections are active homebuyers, making them warm leads who are ready to connect. Zillow's data shows that agents using Premier Agent close 60% more transactions than those who don’t. For testimonials from our agents,
see below
.
How do I create a Premier Agent account on Zillow?
To create a Premier Agent account,
connect with a Zillow business advisor
to determine which Premier Agent program is right for you. We'll provide step-by-step instructions to set up your account and benefits.
How do Premier Agent contracts work?
Contracts are not required, but there are benefits to signing a contract like discounts and prioritized access to new inventory. We offer 6-month contracts as that is the minimum recommendation of time to see the most value out of your Zillow investment.
