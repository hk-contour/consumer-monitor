OUR PURPOSE
Snap was built differently.
From the beginning, the idea was simple: what if communicating online felt more like being together in real life? A place for real conversations between friends, not broadcasting.
This reflects a clear point of view about what technology should do for people. Everything we do at Snap starts with humanity.
