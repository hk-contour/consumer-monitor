Management Team
Evan Spiegel
,
Chief Executive Officer
Mr. Spiegel is our co-founder and has served as our Chief Executive Officer and a member of our board of directors since May 2012. Mr. Spiegel holds a B.S. in Engineering – Product Design from Stanford University. Previously, Mr. Spiegel served on the board of directors of KKR & Co., Inc.
Robert Murphy
,
Chief Technology Officer
Mr. Murphy is our co-founder and has served as our Chief Technology Officer and a member of our board of directors since May 2012. Mr. Murphy holds a B.S. in Mathematical and Computational Science from Stanford University.
Doug Hott
,
Chief Financial Officer
Mr. Hott has served as Chief Financial Officer since May 2026 and previously served as our Vice President of Finance, Strategy, and Corporate Development since July 2024. Prior to that role, Mr. Hott served as our Vice President of Finance from August 2019 to July 2024. Mr. Hott holds a B.S. in Physics from Bradley University, and an M.S. in Astrophysics and an M.B.A. from the University of Cincinnati.
Julie Henderson
,
Chief Communications Officer
Ms. Henderson has served as Chief Communications Officer since April 2019. From July 2013 to April 2019, Ms. Henderson served as Executive Vice President, Chief Communications Officer at Twenty-First Century Fox, Inc. Ms. Henderson also previously served in roles at News Corporation, including as Senior Vice President, Corporate Affairs and Chief Communications Officer, and Senior Vice President, Communications and Corporate Strategy. Ms. Henderson holds a B.A. from Johnston Center, University of Redlands.
Betsy Kenny Lack
,
Vice President, Global Brand Experience
Ms. Lack has served as Vice President, Global Brand Experience since October 2021 and previously served as Head of Global Brand Strategy from July 2016 to October 2021. Prior to that, Ms. Lack was Contributing Editor at Vanity Fair, overseeing the magazine’s Silicon Valley and technology coverage, and created and ran the New Establishment Summit series. Prior to that, Ms. Lack served as a philanthropic and public policy adviser to individuals and companies. Ms. Lack serves on the board of directors of the Snap Foundation, Lincoln Center Theater, and the Walden Woods Project. Ms. Lack is also a Life Trustee of WNET, the organization that oversees public television stations in the New York metropolitan area. Ms. Lack holds a B.A. from Princeton University.
Ajit Mohan
,
Chief Business Officer
Mr. Mohan has served as our Chief Business Officer since February 2025 and previously served as our President of APAC from January 2023 to February 2025. From 2019 to November 2022, Mr. Mohan served as Vice President and Managing Director of India for Meta, where he was responsible for strategy and operations in India, and from April 2016 to December 2018, Mr. Mohan served as CEO of 21st Century Fox’s digital streaming service, Hotstar. Mr. Mohan holds a BASc from Nanyang Technological University Singapore, an M.A. from Johns Hopkins University, and an M.B.A. from The Wharton School at the University of Pennsylvania.
Rebecca Morrow
,
Chief Accounting Officer
Ms. Morrow has served as our Chief Accounting Officer since September 2019. From January 2018 to August 2019, Ms. Morrow served as Chief Accounting Officer at GoDaddy Inc., and previously served as Vice President of Finance and Head of Technical Accounting and Reporting from March 2015 to January 2018. Prior to that, Ms. Morrow served in various roles at Deloitte & Touche LLP, most recently serving as Managing Director in the Advisory Services practice from August 2013 to March 2015, and as Senior Manager in the Advisory Services practice from October 2008 to August 2013. Ms. Morrow holds a B.S. degree in Business and Accounting from the University of Idaho and a Masters of Accountancy degree from the David Eccles School of Business of the University of Utah.
Ms. Morrow reports to Chief Financial Officer Doug Hott.
Zach Briers
,
General Counsel
Mr. Briers has served as our General Counsel since November 2025. From August 2012 to October 2025, Mr. Briers was a lawyer in private practice at the law firm of Munger, Tolles & Olson LLP, where he focused his practice on advising companies and founders on litigation and transactional matters. Mr. Briers holds a J.D. from the New York University School of Law and a B.A. from John Carroll University.
Scott Withycombe
,
Chief People Officer
Mr. Withycombe has served as our Chief People Officer since October 2022 and previously served in various roles, including Vice President of Talent & Rewards, Senior Director of Talent & Rewards, Senior Director of Talent Management, Senior Director of Human Resources, and Human Resources Director between November 2017 and August 2022. Mr. Withycombe also previously served in roles at DirectTV, Raytheon Company, and Del Monte Foods, Inc. Mr. Withycombe holds a B.A. in Economics and Political Science from Pepperdine University and M.S. in International Employment Relations and Human Resource Management from The London School of Economics and Political Science.

Grace  Kao
,
Chief Marketing Officer
With over 20 years of marketing and advertising experience, Grace is Snap’s Chief Marketing Officer, enabling brands around the world to connect with Snap’s 850+ million monthly users in meaningful and effective ways. She has led award-winning campaigns for iconic brands such as Pepsi, PlayStation, Crate&Barrel, Apple. Most recently, she led Spotify Advertising’s ‘Spreadbeats’ B2B campaign, which was one of the most awarded campaigns in 2024, across multiple categories for consumer and business at Cannes, including a Grand Prix. Grace was also named on Adweek’s Top 50 Indispensable Business Leaders. Before joining Snap, she served as Head of Global Business Marketing for Spotify and Instagram across businesses and creators.
Grace has a master’s degree in Communications from Stanford University and resides in San Francisco, California with her husband, two children and cat.
