Is there a free trial and can I cancel later?
Square Plus and Square Premium each offer a free 30-day trial, and you can cancel or switch plans at any point. With Square Free, there are no monthly subscription costs — you only pay processing fees when you take a payment.
Can I accept any type of credit card?
You can accept every major card — Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover — all for the same processing fees. You can also take cash, checks, and Square Gift Cards.
Do I need to purchase hardware?
You do not need to purchase hardware. There are several ways to take payments without any hardware, including invoicing, payment links, virtual terminal, Tap to Pay on iPhone, and Tap to Pay on Android.
