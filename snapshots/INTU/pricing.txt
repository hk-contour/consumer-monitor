Frequently asked questions
Is this online, or do I download software?
QuickBooks Online is accessible securely from any web browser or through the mobile app. No download of software is required.
What is the difference between QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Desktop?
QuickBooks Online is a subscription-based product that gives you the freedom to work on the go. You can access and sync data across all your devices – computer, tablet and smartphone. Your data is securely backed up and you have instant access to the latest product and feature updates. Additionally, you can connect QuickBooks Online to over 300 apps you already use, and save even more time.
QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise
has advanced inventory management, advanced reporting, pricing rules, job costing, payroll, time tracking, and more. QuickBooks Enterprise can also help you with multi-company management by using a single dashboard to track and manage intercompany transactions and view combined reporting. Availability of QuickBooks Enterprise features and functionality vary by subscription type. For details, please view the
QuickBooks Desktop Enterprise Pricing Page
.
Can I upgrade between plans?
You can upgrade between QuickBooks Online Simple Start, Essentials, or Plus.
Can I switch plans or cancel at any time?
Yes, you're free to switch QuickBooks Online plans or cancel at any time. Intuit Experts customers may cancel plans at any time.
Is there a mobile app I can use?
QuickBooks Lite plans and above come with a free mobile app that helps you run your business on the go—anytime, anywhere.
Is it easy to get started?
Absolutely. It only takes about five minutes to get up and running, once you sync your bank or credit card transactions to QuickBooks Online.
Is phone support included?
For Simple Start plans and above, phone support is free during the 30-day trial and included with your paid subscription to QuickBooks Online. Your subscription must be current.
Can I add my accountant, bookkeeper, or tax pro?
Yes, you can invite an accountant to collaborate with you for free with QuickBooks Online. Once they accept your invitation, they can work in your account. You can delete their access anytime.
Is the cost of QuickBooks tax deductible?
Yes! QuickBooks is tax deductible according to IRS publication 946, which states “off-the-shelf computer software is qualifying property for purposes of the section 179 deduction.”
What’s included in Intuit Expert Full Service Bookkeeping?
Intuit Expert Full Service Bookkeeping includes two phases: cleanup and ongoing bookkeeping
In the cleanup/setup phase, your bookkeeper helps you set up your chart of accounts, connects your banks, and teaches you the basics of QuickBooks. If you have information in QuickBooks already, your bookkeeper will help clean it up in the first month of service.
As part of ongoing bookkeeping, your bookkeeper categorizes your transactions and reconciles your accounts each month.
Your bookkeeper can close your books and prepare a trial balance. They can also send PDF copies of key financial reports including your profit and loss statement and balance sheet.
