[MATERIAL] Robinhood is introducing new CAT (Consolidated Audit Trail) pass-through fees effective immediately: $0.0003 per options contract, $0.000003 per share for listed equities, and $0.00000003 per share for OTC equities, reversing prior language stating regulators were no longer charging CAT fees as of December 1, 2025. Additionally, a new "Professional customer" options fee of $0.50 per contract will be implemented on or after October 15, 2026.
