Frequently asked Toast pricing questions
What are the upfront costs?
The upfront costs for Toast are hardware and implementation, which vary depending on your specific hardware packages and installation needs. 0% interest financing is available by application and subject to approval.
Within our Starter Package, we also offer the Pay-as-You-Go plan that reduces your upfront hardware and installation costs through an all-in-one platform rate.
Can I use my own hardware?
You may access Toast's services only through devices identified by Toast as compatible with and capable of accessing or supporting Toast's services using a wired or wireless connection to the internet. Toast's services do not function with every device and may only be used on approved Toast hardware. Toast may alter which devices are approved as compatible with its services in Toast’s discretion from time to time.
Is Toast a payment card processor?
Toast is not a payment card processor, but it is a payment facilitator that partners with processors to offer card processing services.
Does Toast pricing include customer support?
Toast Care is included in every software subscription at no additional cost and open all day, every day — 24/7/365 — by phone or web messaging.
