Corporate Overview
StubHub is the global destination for consumers to access live events and experiences. We envision a future where all live event tickets are widely available to be conveniently purchased and every seat at every venue is filled.
Today, we believe we operate the largest global secondary ticketing marketplace for live events. We connect fans around the world with sellers who use our marketplace to reach passionate fans and price tickets efficiently. We operate our global ticketing marketplace through two brands: StubHub in North America and viagogo internationally.
