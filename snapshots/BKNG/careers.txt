How We Hire
At Booking Holdings we believe that our strength lies in our people, and success is contingent upon our ability to work as one team to succeed in our mission to make it easier for everyone to experience the world. We are continuously seeking candidates to join our global enterprise who share our passion for innovation, collaboration, and excellence. If you’re interested in starting your journey with Booking Holdings, learn more about our interview process below:
After you’ve browsed through the available opportunities across the company that are currently listed on our Careers page, if a role stands out to you, simply click on ‘Apply Now’.
This call is really about you. We want to learn more about your experience, expectations and what makes you shine. This is the perfect opportunity for you to share your motivation for applying and what drives you to succeed. You’ll also get to know us even better and determine if Booking Holdings is for you.
This stage is all about us getting to know each other. We recognize that choosing your next adventure is no small task. Depending on the department and seniority of the role, you may have several rounds of interviews. Interviews may include an assessment of your technical expertise, as well as conversations about other skills crucial for the role.
Your recruiter will collect feedback from your interviewers and be in touch with you promptly to discuss the outcome. In most cases, you should expect to hear back from your recruiter within a week from your interview.
Beware of job scams
Fake job offer scams are unfortunately on the rise. Like many well-known companies, Booking Holdings may have its name misused by scammers to appear legitimate. These fraudulent offers often promise high salaries for minimal work and require little to no formal hiring process. Once you have engaged with such a post, scammers may ask for upfront payments for “training” or “administration” fees, or request that you complete tasks that ultimately lead to financial loss.
Booking Holdings will never:
• Request payment from candidates for any part of the hiring process
• Ask for credit card information via phone, email, WhatsApp, or social media
If you receive a suspicious offer, we recommend verifying its legitimacy through official channels before taking any action.
