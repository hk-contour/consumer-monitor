Corporate Overview
Somnigroup International is the world’s largest bedding company, dedicated to enriching people’s lives through the power of a good night’s sleep. With superior capabilities in design, manufacturing, distribution and retail, we deliver breakthrough sleep solutions and serve the evolving needs of consumers in more than 100 countries worldwide through our fully-owned businesses, Tempur Sealy, Mattress Firm and Dreams.
