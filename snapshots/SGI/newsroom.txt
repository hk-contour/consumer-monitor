Who We Are
Somnigroup International is the world’s largest bedding company,
dedicated to enriching people’s lives through the power of a good night’s sleep.
Our purpose is to drive long-term returns through sustainable competitive
advantages and disciplined capital allocation as we oversee our investments in the
$120 billion global sleep industry.
Somnigroup’s fully-owned businesses include
Tempur Sealy, Mattress Firm and Dreams, which collectively serve the evolving needs
of consumers in
more than 100 countries worldwide.
We are guided by our core value of Doing the Right Thing and committed to our
global responsibility to protect the environment and the communities in which we operate.
What We Do
With superior capabilities in design, manufacturing, distribution and retail, our businesses deliver breakthrough sleep solutions to improve the lives of our global consumers. We bring
over a century of knowledge and industry-leading innovation, with award-winning brands such as
Tempur-Pedic®, Sealy® and Stearns & Foster®.
Our
global omni-channel platform and extensive consumer touchpoints enable us to meet consumers wherever they shop, offering a personal connection and innovation to provide a
unique retail experience and tailored solutions.
