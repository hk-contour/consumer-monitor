About Us
Warner Music Group is home to the world’s most visionary recording artists and iconic labels such as Atlantic, Parlophone, and Warner Records, representing over 75 years of music history across genres, geographies, and generations. Warner Chappell Music, our music publishing business, boasts a robust catalog of over one million copyrights spanning the standards of the Great American Songbook to the biggest hits of the 21st century.
A global collective of music makers and music lovers, our mission is to turn dreams into stardom and audiences into fans. Powered by curious, Independent Minds, our world-class experts help artists and songwriters cut through the clutter, delivering Major Sound.
We believe that innovation and music have always gone hand in hand, and we’re using our future-thinking approach to strengthen global services to artists and songwriters and set WMG up as the model of the modern music company.
